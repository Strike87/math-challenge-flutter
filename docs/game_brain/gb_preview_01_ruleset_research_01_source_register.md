## Discovery-level extraction notes

These notes retain source-reported or source-implied evidence requirements, assumptions, limitations, synthetic applicability, and abstention boundary without extracting a candidate rule. No reported threshold or predicate is adopted in this batch.

Tier basis: the frozen hierarchy names systematic reviews, meta-analyses, peer-reviewed methodological standards, and validated educational-measurement literature as Tier A. R01 and R12 are conservatively Tier C because they are scoping reviews, not systematic reviews or meta-analyses; their discovery value does not elevate them under that hierarchy. X02 is Tier A because it is explicitly a systematic review.

| Source group | Evidence requirements / assumptions identified at discovery | Uncertainty and limitations | Synthetic-preview applicability and abstention boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| R01-R04 and X02 (challenge/difficulty/failure) | Studies operationalize instructional designs, task complexity/load, learner expertise, and learning/transfer outcomes; those constructs require context beyond raw performance. | Measures and effects vary by design and population; direct performance-only proxy is not established. | `PreviewEvidence` lacks intervention, task-complexity, learner-expertise, and delayed outcome data. Abstain from equating its counts/latency to an instructional or learning construct. |
| R05-R06 (recent change) | Repeated comparable measurements, a valid responsive measure, an error/reliability model, and handling of practice effects are required before reliable-change interpretation. | Observed difference can reflect error or practice; method choices require justification. | The frozen input does not supply those measurement facts. Abstain from a measurement-error-resolved improvement claim. |
| R07 (sparse proportions) | Proportion inference depends on the sampling/model assumptions and an interval method suited to the data; large-sample approximations can be poor in small samples. | This is estimation methodology, not a semantic interpretation of a learner or scenario. | Counts alone do not establish independent/binomial observations or validate an uncertainty method. Abstain from importing an interval-derived semantic rule. |
| R08-R09 (reject option) | Reject-option methods normally require a prediction target plus risk, calibration, loss, or evaluation assumptions; ambiguity and novelty are distinct motivations. | The sources concern trained classifiers and can trade prediction quality against rejection rate. | No predictive model, calibration, loss, probability, or target is allowed here. Retain abstention as a research construct only; abstain from classifier-style precedence. |
| R10-R11 (educational measurement) | A score interpretation/use needs theory and evidence linking the observed measure to the intended claim; computer-based response-time/log-data analyses use measurement models. | Validity is argument- and use-specific; a score may contain construct-irrelevant variance. | The synthetic fields have no validity argument for challenge, learning, or diagnosis. Abstain whenever that interpretive link is absent. |
| R12 (uncertainty/conflict) | Conflicting, ambiguous, and scarce evidence are distinguishable sources of uncertainty in a decision context. | Medical conceptual taxonomy is not an educational or classifier decision procedure. | Can inform future construct separation only. Abstain from treating this taxonomy as a precedence or compatibility rule. |
