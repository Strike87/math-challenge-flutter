## Evidence gaps and conflicts retained

- No discovered source validates the frozen aggregate counts and categorical synthetic latency as an operational measure of productive challenge, overchallenge, or underchallenge.
- No discovered source transfers a reliable-change calculation to two synthetic count segments without a measurement model, reliability data, repeated comparable administrations, and treatment of practice effects.

- Reject-option literature supports abstention as a distinct decision concept, but its calibration, loss, and probability assumptions do not map to the contract and cannot settle ambiguity or precedence.
- The literature families are complementary but not commensurable: testing validity governs the support for score interpretation; precision methods govern statistical estimation; instructional studies govern learning-design effects; classifier sources govern rejection architectures. They must not be averaged, merged, or treated as a single evidence scale.
